How to Install PennyFlow on Android

PennyFlow is 100% free and open-source. You can download and install the signed APK directly onto your phone with zero Google Play hassle.

1

Download APK

Tap the "Download Free Android APK" button. Your browser (Chrome) will begin downloading the official PennyFlow-v1.0.0.apk file from GitHub.

2

Open Downloaded File

When the download finishes, swipe down your notification tray or open your phone's Downloads folder and tap the APK file.

3

Allow Unknown Apps

If Android prompts "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps", tap Settings and toggle on "Allow from this source".

4

Tap Install & Launch

Tap Install. In just a few seconds, PennyFlow will be ready. Open it, configure your monthly budget, and enjoy effortless money tracking!

Why install via APK? By distributing our open-source release directly through GitHub, PennyFlow remains 100% free, free from corporate tracking, and completely independent of third-party store commissions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about security, offline storage, and open-source updates.

Is PennyFlow really 100% free and open source?
Yes, completely! PennyFlow is released under the permissive MIT License. There are no monthly subscriptions, no paywalled features, and zero advertisements. All source code is publicly accessible on GitHub.
Do I need to link or connect my bank account?
No! Never. Traditional finance apps require sensitive bank credentials that can be compromised. PennyFlow uses fast manual input, receipt OCR scanning, and smart calculations without ever touching your bank logins.
Does PennyFlow work offline without an internet connection?
Yes! All your daily budgeting, Safe-to-Spend calculations, expense entries, and receipt logs work completely offline on your device storage. You only need internet if you choose to sync with your private Supabase cloud database.
How does the "Penny" AI Financial Coach work?
Penny analyzes your spending patterns, pacing against your monthly budget, and burn rate. You can switch between Pragmatic mode for balanced advice, Roast mode for hilarious reality checks, or Hype mode for celebration.
Will updating the APK delete my saved expenses and receipts?
No! Android preserves the app's internal persistent database and local storage during updates. When you install a newer release APK over an existing one, all your financial history remains 100% safe.
Can I try the app without installing the APK on my phone?
Yes! You can launch the interactive Web App Demo right in your desktop or mobile browser by clicking "Web Demo" at the top or visiting app.html.
